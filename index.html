Regenerative Denoise & Upscale — ComfyUI + Flux

A ComfyUI workflow that restores degraded animation using diffusion-based inpainting. A signal processing chain (Canny, FBCNN, bilateral filtering) constructs a per-frame damage mask, and FLUX.1-fill-dev regenerates masked regions guided by ControlNet lineart — recovering detail that compression destroyed.

Pipeline
01
Signal Extraction — Mask Construction
Manga2Anime lineart, dual Canny passes (soft 30/85 and hard 170/255), and FBCNN artifact simulation blended with multiply operations into a composite grayscale mask. Bright = damaged. Dark = clean.
02
Diffusion Regeneration
FLUX.1-fill-dev inpaints masked regions guided by ControlNet Union Pro lineart (strength 0.55, released at 75%). Differential Diffusion applies spatially-varying noise by mask value. Guidance at 2.0.
03
Post-Processing & Output
UltimateSDUpscale 1.5× at denoise=0.01 (tile sharpening only), bilateral filter, brightness/contrast normalization. RIFE 4.7 doubles frame rate. Encoded H.264 MP4 at CRF 19.
